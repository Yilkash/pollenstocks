## Two-minute recording

1. **Problem:** “Freelancers need a simple way to pay collaborators and keep track of what actually happened.”
2. **Balance:** Connect the wallet and ask, “What is my balance?” Show SERV returning live chain data.
3. **Payment:** Ask, “Send 5 Demo USD to Sila.” Show the resolved name, full address, token, amount and fee.
4. **Approval:** Approve the reviewed transfer in the wallet.
5. **Proof:** Show the matching onchain receipt, updated chat status and payment history.
6. **Persistence:** Reload and show the same session/history.
7. **Control:** Explain that the AI prepares drafts while the wallet authorizes transfers; ambiguous outcomes require recovery instead of automatic resending.

Optional extra clip: a recorded or clearly labelled local-fixture cancellation/replacement recovery. Do not present mocked test results as live transactions.
